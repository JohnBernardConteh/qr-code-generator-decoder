\# QR Code Generator \& Decoder



An app for generating QR codes from text or URLs and decoding QR codes from uploaded images.



\[!\[Open in Streamlit](https://static.streamlit.io/badges/streamlit\_badge\_black\_white.svg)](https://jbc-qr-code-generator-decoder-app.streamlit.app/)



\## Features



\- Generate QR codes from text or URLs.

\- Download generated QR codes as PNG images.

\- Decode uploaded JPG, JPEG, and PNG images.

\- Download decoded text.

\- View QR detection details.
